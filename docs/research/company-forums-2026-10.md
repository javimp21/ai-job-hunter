# What engineers and candidates say: 24 target companies (research, 2026-10-05)

Follow-up to [junior-companies-2026-10.md](junior-companies-2026-10.md), whose reputation evidence came from search-engine snippets of Glassdoor, Blind and levels.fyi. This report goes to the underlying pages wherever I could read them.

Profile: junior backend engineer, about 1 year of Java/Spring Boot, English B2, Spanish native, based in Madrid, open to the Netherlands, Ireland, Luxembourg and Switzerland.

**Read this first.**

- **Reddit was blocked** (details in the last section), so none of it is used, directly or through proxies. Glassdoor, Indeed, Mediavida, GoWork and Medium were also unreadable. The reputation evidence comes mainly from **Blind** (public company review pages and threads), **Forocoches** (Spanish threads), **levels.fyi** (pages updated 2026-10-05), **Jointaro**, a few Hacker News and news items, and the **companies' own job postings** (read through their public ATS APIs on 2026-10-05).
- Blind reviews are anonymous, global (not tied to Madrid, Dublin or Amsterdam unless the text says so) and self-declared. Salary numbers on Blind and levels.fyi are self-reported. **levels.fyi figures for Spain, Switzerland and some NL pages are medians over all levels, not junior pay**; I say so wherever that is the case. Submission counts on levels.fyi are company-wide.
- Evidence about juniors specifically is thin almost everywhere. Where a source is a junior-level reviewer (for example 'Software Engineer I'), I say so. Everything else is evidence about engineers in general.
- I do not name reviewers, forum users or employees, and quote only a few words. Everything else is paraphrased.
- Dates matter: a 2021 forum thread is not 2026. Each claim carries its year.
- The verdict is for this profile today: **apply** (a real junior-compatible path exists and the evidence is reasonable), **apply with caution** (path exists but the evidence shows a real downside, or the path is weak), **avoid for a junior** (no junior-compatible path today, or the evidence says the role would not serve a junior). 'Avoid' is about this profile now, not a judgement of the company.

## Company by company

Pay conventions: 'levels.fyi total' means base plus stock plus bonus as reported on [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies), pages updated 2026-10-05. 'Blind rating' is the overall rating shown on the company's Blind page on 2026-10-05.

### 1. Stripe (Dublin, also Barcelona)

- **Verdict: apply with caution.** Best pay and a posting you qualify for, but the 2026 reviews describe an intense, stack-ranked environment.
- **Junior path (first party).** 'Software Engineer, New Grad' in Dublin, updated 2026-09-25: degree by summer 2027 or equivalent work experience, and a new grad has **no more than 18 months of professional experience (internships excluded)**; stack Java, Ruby, JavaScript, Scala, Go; mentioned learning through a mentor. [posting](https://stripe.com/jobs/search?gh_jid=8130881). The posting text I read says nothing about relocation or remote policy.
- **What engineers say.**
  - Good: reviewers in September 2026 say engineers get a lot of work and responsibility, which helps growth, pay is good and 'WLB isn't terrible' ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Stripe/reviews)). A 2019 personal blog by a former engineer describes classes in the first two weeks and a two-week starter project with a mentor ([blog](https://leportella.com/new-eng-stripe/); stale, written about San Francisco onboarding for a Dublin hire).
  - Bad: the same 2026 reviews describe stack ranking, many people performance-managed out in 2026, on-call toil and constant reorganisations in at least one team, and managers messaging outside hours ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Stripe/reviews)). A 2021 thread says Ireland work-life balance was poor but pay was very good ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/Stripe-Ireland-WLB-mKDcD7i1)).
- **Junior pay.** levels.fyi L1 Dublin EUR 110k total, base EUR 72.6k ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/stripe/salaries/software-engineer/locations/greater-dublin-area)); a May 2024 thread has a signed Dublin new grad quoting about EUR 105k total ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/meta-vs-stripe-dublin-ireland-qsr6vkvy)). The previous report said about EUR 116k; the page now shows EUR 110k.
- **Interview.** For new grads, accounts describe an online assessment, then a team screen with a practical (not LeetCode-style) problem built in several stages, then an onsite; a US sample is quoted with a low pass rate from only 16 reports ([Jointaro](https://www.jointaro.com/interviews/companies/stripe/experiences/software-engineer-new-grad-united-states-november-5-2025-no-offer-neutral-d8d78bbd/)). Not Dublin-specific.
- **Layoffs.** About 300 people in January 2025 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/21/stripe-is-laying-off-300-people-but-says-it-still-plans-to-hire-in-2025)); around 90 in Dublin in January 2023 per a Hacker News comment quoting press ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=34390538)).
- **Red flags.** Performance culture; 'ever-changing priorities' in one team's review; relocation and remote terms not stated.

### 2. Adyen (Amsterdam; Madrid office)

- **Verdict: apply with caution.** Java-heavy and well paid, but opinions on culture and growth are polarised and there is no junior-labelled posting.
- **Junior path (first party).** Open Java roles in Amsterdam, for example [Java Software Engineer - Payments](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/adyen/jobs/7369512) (updated 2026-09-15) and a [GenAI Platform Engineer in Madrid or Amsterdam](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/adyen/jobs/8163866) (2026-10-05). Both say Adyen is an **office-first company and does not offer remote-only roles**. A Hacker News hiring post (July 2026) for a senior Madrid role mentions 60% on-site and relocation within Europe ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48819384)).
- **What engineers say.**
  - Good: Blind reviews from August-September 2026 repeatedly praise work-life balance ('one of the best'), one engineer reviewer likes the people and the modernising stack ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Adyen/reviews)). A 2024 former engineer rated work-life balance 5/5 ([Jointaro](https://www.jointaro.com/interviews/companies/adyen/work-experiences/software-engineer-amsterdam-july-18-2024-4-6149548e/)).
  - Bad: a September 2026 engineer review says career growth is very hard and attrition is not acknowledged by leadership; another calls pay low with terrible CI/CD and legacy code; a December 2024 thread has a self-described current Amsterdam employee advising against joining and another calling it cult-like, while a third reply says most people are happy ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/adyen-salary-and-work-culture-adyen-z3vxur21), [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/adyen-salary-expectation-5dwgs0aa)). Older Jointaro reviews (2020, 2024) call the tech environment dated and grueling for developers.
- **Junior pay.** levels.fyi Software Engineer 1 in Amsterdam EUR 93.3k total, base EUR 76.5k ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/adyen/salaries/software-engineer/locations/greater-amsterdam-area)). Blind comments for new grads in January 2025 recall EUR 60-65k base plus a EUR 12k bonus plus shares (and a 2022 comment says EUR 70-75k total); the January 2025 commenter adds that raises barely beat inflation once you are in ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/adyen-amsterdam-salary-graduate-data-platform-engineer-strcrxse), [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/new-grad-salaries-in-amsterdam-tl0fnbty)). The two sources differ by about EUR 15k at base; I would plan on the lower one for a first offer.
- **Interview.** A US account (August 2026) and a Madrid account (July 2025) both describe a HackerRank-style task followed by a discussion of the submission with a focus on concurrency ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/adyen-interview-experience-nozyd51k), [Jointaro](https://www.jointaro.com/interviews/companies/adyen/work-experiences/software-engineer-amsterdam-july-18-2024-4-6149548e/)). Brush up on Java concurrency.
- **Layoffs.** None found in the sources I could read. **English.** Postings are in English. **Relocation.** Only the senior Madrid post above mentions it.
- **Red flags.** Office-first rule; the split reviews; hires may be graded Engineer I or II, which moves base pay by roughly EUR 15-20k (Blind, January 2025).

### 3. CaixaBank Tech (Madrid, Barcelona, Sevilla)

- **Verdict: apply with caution.** A real junior Java opening, but forum users describe a consultancy-style unit.
- **Junior path (first party).** 'Software Engineer Java + Python (Junior)', permanent contract, Madrid, Barcelona and Sevilla, listed under a 'Tech Services' clan ([posting](https://www.caixabanktech.com/es/job/junior-software-engineer-java-python/)); the previous report read hybrid with up to 60% remote and a 6-month minimum experience on the same page. The page was intermittently unreachable today.
- **What engineers say.** A Forocoches thread (September 2024 to November 2025) is the only readable source: several users say IT staff were moved into this subsidiary, call it bank-in-name-only with consultancy conditions and a long commute to the Las Rozas office (Madrid), one who works with them says the pressure comes from subcontracting chains but the atmosphere is good; a candidate in November 2025 withdrew after the technical interview because the stack did not convince them and the office (two days a week) was 30 km away; another asks whether salaries are high and gets the answer that 'irresistible' offers are EUR 40-50k in real life ([Forocoches](https://forocoches.com/foro/showthread.php?t=10108530)). A user in January 2025 says BBVA Technology looks more interesting than the competition.
- **Junior pay.** No junior figure in a readable source. levels.fyi shows a median of EUR 43.9k across levels for CaixaBank in Spain ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/caixabank/salaries/software-engineer)), which is not a junior number.
- **Red flags.** 'Tech Services' consultancy framing (my inference from the clan name plus the forum thread); only anecdotal evidence; the previous report's remark about no inflation raises came from a review aggregator and is **not** confirmed by a readable source here.

### 4. Indra / Minsait (Madrid)

- **Verdict: apply with caution.** A classic first job: easy to get, low start, slow raises. Negotiate the starting salary and leave after 1-2 years.
- **Junior path (first party).** 'Desarrollador/a Junior Java' in Madrid, under 2 years, Spring Boot, hybrid (previous report, [posting](https://careers.indragroup.com/job/Madrid-Desarrolladora-Junior-Java-MD/978237755/)).
- **What engineers say.** Forocoches threads:
  - 2021: a junior Spring Boot + Angular offer from EUR 21k with 100% remote; replies call it fine for a first job, project-dependent, 'a good place to build a CV', with slow salary growth ([thread](https://forocoches.com/foro/showthread.php?t=8717120)); another thread says juniors start at EUR 24-26k and raises are EUR 500 every six months in the Smart Start programme ([thread](https://forocoches.com/foro/showthread.php?t=8838227)).
  - October 2024: a Smart Start entrant went from EUR 26k to EUR 26.6k at six months; others report raises around EUR 500-650 a year; one former employee entered at EUR 19k, got EUR 1k every six months, was still 'junior' at EUR 22k after two years and left ([thread](https://forocoches.com/foro/showthread.php?t=10134228)).
  - October 2025: for a consultant role at Minsait, users say everything depends on the division, project and manager; one has worked remotely for three years and is happy; another notes six-month probation periods in consultancy contracts ([thread](https://forocoches.com/foro/showthread.php?t=10490991)).
  - Two Blind reviews of Minsait (2020, 2022) say it is a good place to start but there is no career progression and you should negotiate pay on entry ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Minsait/reviews)).
- **Junior pay.** About EUR 19-26k at entry in the threads above (2021-2024); levels.fyi shows a median EUR 36.3k across levels in Spain, 71 submissions ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/indra/salaries/software-engineer)). These two are not comparable.
- **Red flags.** Project lottery; small raises; possible placement at public-sector clients (one user describes a ministry project with little work); old threads.

### 5. Celonis (Madrid, Munich, Amsterdam)

- **Verdict: avoid for a junior (backend).** The only early-career path is customer-facing, and the stability signals are poor.
- **Junior path (first party).** The 'Orbit' graduate programme in Madrid is for Associate Solution Consultants (customer-facing, not backend): Madrid for two years, then a possible move to Amsterdam; mentoring and coaching, hybrid model, unlimited PTO; the Benelux variant asks for English and Dutch ([posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/celonis/jobs/7885977003)).
- **What engineers say.** Blind rating 3.3/5 from 103 reviews. Engineers (2024-2026; only one reviewer says Munich, locations are mostly not stated) like work-life balance and the office, but say salary stagnated, shares are worthless until an IPO, raises are small, management is lost, and in May 2026 one cites frequent executive departures and a false sense of urgency ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Celonis/reviews)).
- **Layoffs.** A February post says QA roles were cut worldwide; a reply from someone who says they work there asks if it is true ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/celonis-laid-off-all-qas-globally-a3m861x2)). Single, unverified claim.
- **Junior pay.** levels.fyi has no entry-level (IC1) data; IC2 in Madrid is EUR 56.5k total ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/celonis/salaries/software-engineer/locations/madrid-metropolitan-area)).
- **Red flags.** Role mismatch; restructurings; shares.

- **Junior path (first party).** 'Graduate Software Engineer' in Spain: a full-time **scholarship of up to EUR 24,000 gross a year**, for final-year students or recent graduates, working in production from day one, English and Spanish classes ([posting](https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/feverup/jobs/4951979101), updated 2026-09-16); a parallel 'Becas de Excelencia - Engineering' offers the same cap ([posting](https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/feverup/jobs/4942287101)). Stack in the previous report was Python/Django.
- **What engineers say.** The only readable forum items are from 2015 and are about management style, so I do not use them ([Forocoches](https://forocoches.com/foro/showthread.php?t=4532853)). Glassdoor pages with reviews were unreadable here.
- **Pay.** EUR 24k cap above; levels.fyi shows a median EUR 56.9k across levels in Spain, 30 submissions ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/fever/salaries/software-engineer)).
- **Red flags.** Scholarship instead of an employment contract at a salary below what a candidate with a year of Java should accept; Python stack.

- **What engineers say.** Blind rating 3.7/5 from only 14 reviews, work-life balance 4.4/5, pay the weakest part (3.1/5). Engineers from 2022-2024 mention good work-life balance, hybrid work, few meetings, relatively low pay, no equity and post-acquisition management drift ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Mendix/reviews)).
- **Pay.** No junior figure. levels.fyi: median EUR 74.1k across levels in the Netherlands, 34 submissions, sample shown is a senior ([levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/companies/mendix/salaries/software-engineer)).
- **Red flags.** No Java; no relocation; thin evidence.

### 10. IMC Trading (Amsterdam)

- **Verdict: apply with caution.** Very high pay and normal hours, but the junior door is a graduate programme you may not qualify for, and the stack is C++/Java.

The previous report read it as aimed at final-year students graduating in 2027. Software engineers in 2026 praise work-life balance and social life, say you can work 'normal hours', and say some weeks can be about 20 hours; complaints: lower pay than the trading industry, secret bonus calculation, poor growth, many 'coasters', and one dislikes the Java layer ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/IMC/reviews)). In 2023 a self-declared IMC engineer reports 40-45 hours a week but says engineers mainly support traders and bonuses are opaque, and another commenter calls the code base hard to maintain and says new people have little room to experiment ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-is-imc-for-swes-really-xtos3yfs)).

A March 2025 thread disputes levels.fyi's accuracy for trading firms ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/quant-devs-at-imc-in-amsterdam-is-levelsfyi-right-xhynchuf)).
- **Red flags.** Eligibility; C++ focus; opaque bonuses.

### 11. Booking.com (Amsterdam)

- **Verdict: apply with caution.**
- **Junior path.** The Compass graduate programme (12 months, rotations, four-week bootcamp, mentor and buddy) is described by Booking on its own site ([programme page](https://careers.booking.com/booking-com-compass-se/), [blog](https://careers.booking.com/blog/our_graduate_software_engineering_programme/); employer-authored, and the site blocks scripted fetches, so I only saw search-engine excerpts). September 2026 engineer reviews: good pay for Europe, no blame culture, great balance, but siloed organisations, politics, 'no growth' and, per one, no internal promotions for three years for 95% of people; two junior ('Software Engineer I') reviewers like the work-life balance (one also the pay), and one of them works on a contract and is unsure of a permanent conversion ([Blind reviews](https://www.teamblind.com/company/Booking.com/reviews)). 2018 threads call internal tooling chaotic and say many developers were relocated immigrants helped by the Dutch 30% tax ruling ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/Hows-life-at-BookingCom-ooVnNED4)); old but consistent with the 'chill but stagnant' picture.

- **Layoffs.** About 1,000 jobs worldwide, between 200 and 1,000 in Amsterdam, announced July 2025 ([DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/07/booking-to-cut-some-1000-jobs-significant-impact-in-amsterdam/)).
- **Relocation.** Not verified for 2026. The 2018 Blind thread says Booking relocated staff then.

## Method and limits

1. For each company I (a) listed open engineering postings in the target countries through the company's public ATS API and read the relevant ones for experience limits, remote, relocation, languages and pay, (b) read Blind's company review page and relevant threads, (c) read levels.fyi summaries, and (d) for Spanish companies read the Forocoches threads that search surfaced, followed by news checks for layoffs.
2. The ranking and verdicts are judgement. Missing evidence is shown as missing, not as negative.
3. No evidence was found about interview process for Indra, CaixaBank Tech, Santander, Cabify, Fever, Nexthink, ARHS, Coolblue, TomTom, Swissquote, Julius Baer, Amadeus or Proton, and none about relocation support beyond the cases named above.
4. Everything is dated 2026-10-05 or earlier; postings and reviews change daily.
