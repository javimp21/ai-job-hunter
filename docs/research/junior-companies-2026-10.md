# Junior-friendly companies and job portals (research, 2026-10-05)

Research for a junior backend profile (about 1 year of Java/Spring Boot, B.Sc. 2026, English B2). Preference order: Madrid first, then Luxembourg, Switzerland, Netherlands, Ireland, then other Spanish cities.

**Read this first.**

- 51 companies are scored, below the 60-120 target. I preferred fewer rows with a checked source over padding with companies I could not evidence.
- Reputation and salary evidence comes from search-engine summaries of Glassdoor, Blind, levels.fyi and trade press. I did not read the review pages themselves. All salary numbers are self-reported and only indicative. Reddit and similar forums were blocked (see limits).
- Junior evidence is stronger: every `junior evidence` cell cites a posting or programme page seen on 2026-10-05, mostly through the companies' public ATS APIs. Postings change daily.
- Scores are judgement calls. Fit = match with this profile (junior title or programme, stack, eligibility, location priority). Conditions = pay vs local market, hours, stability, sentiment. Confidence = amount and recency of evidence. Ranking score = 0.5*fit + 0.35*conditions + a small bonus for confidence.
- Several postings exist only as 'student' internships or final-year-student programmes; those are flagged because the candidate already has a degree and a year of experience.
- Eligibility assumption (not verified): the candidate holds an EU passport, so no visa is needed in LU, NL, IE or CH (Swiss permits still apply). No source about visa or relocation support was verified per company.

## What the evidence says about the target markets

- **Spain / Madrid.** Real junior Java postings exist at Indra (Spring Boot, <2 years, hybrid) and CaixaBank Tech (Java + Python, 6 months minimum, 60% remote), but junior pay in services and bank IT is low (about EUR 21-27k per Glassdoor/Indeed summaries). Product companies pay more but rarely post junior titles; Fever's graduate role is a 6-month scholarship at up to EUR 24k, which is below what a candidate with a year of experience should accept. Datadog Madrid reports EUR 65-75k for 1-3 years but only an internship is open. A Spanish pay guide lists Meta, Datadog, Microsoft, Celonis, Revolut, Glovo and Amazon as top payers in Spain [seniorstack](https://seniorstack.es/guia-salarios-tech-espana-2026/); it is a search summary, not verified.
- **Netherlands.** Adyen (Java) and Booking.com (Compass graduate programme) combine graduate packages around EUR 60-75k with English as working language. IMC pays far more but its posting asks for final-year students graduating in 2027. Mendix has junior postings with lower pay.
- **Ireland.** Stripe's new-grad track is the standout (Dublin median entry package about EUR 116k per levels.fyi); outside Stripe, EUR 100k at entry is not realistic according to a trade guide [eurotoptech](https://www.eurotoptech.com/blog/software-engineer-salary-ireland-2026). Several Dublin employers show layoffs or weak engineer sentiment.
- **Luxembourg / Switzerland.** Few explicit junior roles. ARHS (consultancy) lists junior ETL/Power BI roles; Swissquote and Proton show CHF 78-110k junior pay in self-reported data, with caveats on sentiment and language (Gland is French-speaking).
- **Forward Deployed Engineer.** Verified postings exist at Palantir (Madrid, Amsterdam), OpenAI (Madrid, Spanish-speaking), Databricks (Amsterdam) and Fever (AI engineer, forward deployed), plus Celonis' Orbit programme and Okta/Auth0's Associate Solutions Engineer in Dublin. None of the first three states a junior level, and I did not research their reputations.

## Method and limits

1. **Candidates.** Spanish and LU/CH/NL/IE employers known for engineering hubs were probed against public ATS APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable) using guessed board slugs; boards were confirmed by the companies' own job titles and locations. Several guessed slugs matched a different company and were discarded (for example a board named `gloat` for Glean, and tiny boards for Grover and Verse).
2. **Junior evidence.** Titles on those boards were filtered for junior/graduate/associate/early-career in the target countries; a few postings were read in full (Fever, Celonis, Mendix, IMC, Nebius, Indra, CaixaBank Tech). Spanish banks and large IT firms without public ATS APIs were checked through the posting pages and press releases found by search. A community GitHub list supplied a few graduate roles.
3. **Reputation and pay.** Web-search summaries of Glassdoor, Blind, levels.fyi and trade press. The summaries were not cross-checked against the underlying pages, so ratings and review counts may be stale or mis-attributed. Some figures looked inconsistent (for example a Glassdoor snippet giving a Mendix junior salary of about 3,181 per year, which I discarded; currencies for some Glassdoor pages are mixed).
4. **Not accessible.** Reddit (search tool blocked reddit.com, direct JSON 403), Welcome to the Jungle (403 for scripted requests), Indeed and Glassdoor review pages (not fetched, only search snippets), Manfred and Spanish Top Tech salary pages (not reached), X/Twitter, and several careers pages that returned 403 or proxy errors (Booking.com, Santalucia, Julius Baer). No Spanish forum thread (Forocoches, Mediavida) was read beyond a snippet that surfaced in search.
5. **Thin evidence.** Santalucia, Inditex, Amadeus, Amazon Madrid, Sage, Julius Baer, Factorial, SES, Elastic, Grafana, Palantir, OpenAI and Databricks have low confidence: either no live junior posting or few reviews. Datadog, Elastic, Grafana and the other fit-2 rows have no junior-labelled engineering posting today; revisit when early-career roles open.
6. **Not covered.** Companies I did not research are not 'bad': Bending Spoons (Valencia), BBVA, Telefonica Tech, Capgemini and NTT DATA postings, university career sites, and many Dutch, Swiss and Luxembourg employers without public ATS boards (for example Optiver, ING, bol, UBS, Clearstream). Translate the lack of data as unknown, not negative.
7. **Interview difficulty, relocation and visa support** were not verified for any company; no figures for these appear above on purpose.
