Research date: 2026-10-05. Source label: `us_remote_spain_2026_10`. Lead file: `config/leads/us-remote-spain-2026-10.json` (62 leads).

## How to read this

- **Verified** means: the company's public job board answered its keyless ATS API on 2026-10-05 and at least one posting's location text contains Spain, Madrid, Barcelona, EMEA, Europe or EU. Nothing else is claimed as verified.
- **Group**: A = remote-tagged Spain/EMEA/Europe/EU posting; B = Madrid/Barcelona office posting without a remote tag; A+B = both.
- **Spain** counts postings whose location text names Spain, Madrid or Barcelona. **EMEA/EU** counts postings that name EMEA, Europe or EU without naming Spain. These are posting counts as returned by the API: the same role listed once per location counts several times, and an `EMEA` tag does not guarantee that Spain-resident hires are accepted (the tag is sometimes attached to a specific other-country office).
- **Relevant titles** are a title filter, not a judgement: engineering/developer/AI/forward-deployed titles without senior, staff, principal, lead, manager, architect or sales words, and without a `3+ years` (or higher) phrase in the posting body. Companies with `-` have Spain/EMEA postings but no junior/mid engineering title today; they stay on the list because the brief asked for every company with a Spain/EMEA posting, ranked lower. Years phrases were matched by simple text scan; always re-read the posting.
- US headquarters is my own assessment of each company (not machine-verified from a source). Companies with unclear or non-US headquarters are in the excluded table, not in the lead file.
- Request pace: one request per second per host. No application, contact or other external action was taken.

## Employment model (own entity, EOR, contractor)

Only GitLab, G2i, Drata and Toptal say anything in plain words (see table). For every other company the posting text and careers pages read do not say whether Spain hires go through an own entity, an EOR (Deel, Remote.com, Oyster) or contractor agreements. Do not assume: ask in the first call. Companies with a Spain office posting (group B) are most likely to hire through a local entity, but that is an inference, not something the pages state.

## Limits

- ATS location text is what companies typed; a `Remote - Spain` tag is strong evidence, a bare `EMEA` tag is weak.
- A board that returned 0 Spain/EMEA postings today is not listed even if the company hires in Spain at other times; re-run before relying on absence.
- Counts and titles are a snapshot of 2026-10-05. Nothing was applied to or contacted.
